It works.

This is your site. Every file you need is in the site/ folder.

Change the words on this page, push your changes, and they appear here within a minute or two. There is nothing to install, nothing to configure, and no account to sign up for.

Start here

  • index.html

    This page. Usually the first thing worth rewriting.

  • assets/style.css

    Colors, spacing, fonts. All of it, in one file.

  • about.html

    A second page, to show how linking between pages works.

Adding a page

Copy about.html, rename it, change what's inside, and add a link to it in the navigation at the top of each page. That's the whole process. There is no build step and no framework to learn.
