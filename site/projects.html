Images

Put images in the assets/ folder next to the stylesheet, then link to them like <img src="/assets/photo.jpg" alt="what it shows">. Always write the alt text. It's what people using a screen reader hear, and it's what shows up if the image fails to load.

Keep photos under about 300 KB each. Large images are the single most common reason a student site feels slow.
